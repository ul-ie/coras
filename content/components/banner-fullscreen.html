The video is a supporting background asset; it is not intended to facilitate story-telling or communicate complex messaging; this should be done via text and/or explicit call-to-actions.

Other important points a background video like this needs to avoid:

  • Fast movement or action, which can be jarring and distract users
  • High contrast or distracting foreground content, which can obscure the main text or CTA
  • Excessive focal elements (e.g. too many close-ups of people talking) which prevents it blending into the background as intended
  • Text or logos (for the same reasons we avoid them on images)
